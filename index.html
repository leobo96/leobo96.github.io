About

The intersection

I studied how people think, decide, and get confused. Then I learned to build the systems that remove that confusion. My psychology background isn't a quirky biographical detail — it's the lens through which I approach every product decision. When I see a 3-step form, I see cognitive load. When I see error messages, I see lost users. When I see a slow deploy, I see a team blocked from shipping the thing that matters. At Plannix, this lens turned a scattered 180-Lambda backend into 4 coherent apps, a 0% test coverage into 1,400+ stories, and a complex financial planning frontend into something users can actually navigate.
